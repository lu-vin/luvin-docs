You can use Luvin without creating a workspace. Open some files, try a workflow and close the app. This is useful for a quick test or to look at a contract someone sent you. Nothing is written to your disk unless you save a file yourself.

## Example: try the Pokédex files

To look at the PokéAPI contract and run a workflow without creating anything, open these files from `examples/pokemon/`:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `pokeapi.openapi.yaml` | The contract of the public PokéAPI |
| `pokedex-events.asyncapi.yaml` | The messaging contract |
| `pokemon.arazzo.yaml` | The four workflows |

The workflows appear in the sidebar. Run **Pokemon card** against the **Real API** as in the [Quick start](/get-started/quick-start/). Edits are not written to disk.

## Open files or a folder

On the welcome screen, at the bottom, click **Open files without a workspace**.

A folder dialog opens. Luvin loads every YAML and JSON file in the folder and its subfolders, as one collection.

If you closed the welcome screen with **I’ll explore on my own**, the screen **No collection is open** has the same options: **Open folder** and **Show the welcome**.

You can also drop `.yaml`, `.yml` or `.json` files on the window. The window says **Drop the files to open them.**

After loading, the workflows appear in the sidebar and you can design, edit and run them as usual.

<Aside type="note" title="The Examples">
The Pokédex and PayPal examples are not a session. They open as collections of a workspace called **Examples**, saved inside Luvin, so they behave like any collection.
</Aside>

## What changes without a workspace

| | With a workspace | Without a workspace |
|---|---|---|
| Where changes go | Into files, automatically. | Nowhere on disk. The editors show **Not saved**. |
| Arazzo edits | Saved by the automatic saving. | Saved only when you save the file from the **YAML** tab: Luvin writes it over its file. |
| Secrets | Stored encrypted on this computer. | Not saved. You type them again. |
| Several collections | Yes. | One, made of the files you opened. |

## The saved session

So that a closed tab does not cost you the work, Luvin saves a **session** on this device half a second after each change. Next time you open the app, it offers to continue.

The session keeps:

- The files you opened, as they are now (contracts and Arazzo, with your edits).
- Contracts you attached by hand and sources that Luvin downloaded from a URL (they are downloaded again when you continue).
- The inputs and the cases.
- The selected workflow, and whether you were on **Real API** or **Simulation**.
- The environments, with their base URLs, variables and colors, and which one was active.

The session leaves out:

- **Credentials**, such as tokens, passwords and API keys. You paste them again.
- **The values of secrets.** Only their names are kept.
- **Any input whose name looks sensitive**, like `token`, `password`, `secret`, `authorization`, `api_key` or `client_secret`.
- Saved requests, the authorization of workflows, the results of runs and the Dev Tools log.

<Aside type="caution">
The session lives in the storage of the app on this computer, not in a folder you can see. To keep your work for real, [create a workspace](/workspaces/create-and-open/).
</Aside>

## Continue or discard

When a session exists, the welcome screen shows a card with the title of that session:

<Steps>

1. Click **Continue where you left off**. The same card appears on the empty screen **No collection is open**.
2. Luvin rebuilds the collection from the saved files and brings back your inputs, cases and environments.
3. Paste your credentials again. If you run against the real API without them, the calls that need them answer 401.

</Steps>

To throw it away, click **Discard**, at the bottom of the welcome screen. The session is deleted.

Opening other files starts a new session and replaces the previous one. While a workspace is open, no session is saved: the workspace has its own files.

## Move to a workspace

Create a workspace, then bring your files in:

<Steps>

1. Create the workspace and a collection (see [Create and open a workspace](/workspaces/create-and-open/) and [Collections](/workspaces/collections/)).
2. Click **Import Arazzo or contracts** on **Home**, or drop the files on the window.
3. Fill the environments again. The values of secrets were not in the session.

</Steps>
