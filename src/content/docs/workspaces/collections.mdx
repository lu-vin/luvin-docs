A collection is a folder inside the workspace with everything about one topic: contracts, Arazzo files, environments, cases and requests. You create one per partner, product or family of APIs. This page covers every action on a collection.

![The workspace home with its collections and the Quick actions](/media/en/home.webp)

## Create a collection

You need an open workspace. See [Create and open a workspace](/workspaces/create-and-open/).

<Steps>

1. Open **Home** (the house icon at the top left). Under **Quick actions**, click **New collection**.
   - Or, in the sidebar, click **+** next to **Collections**.
   - On a new workspace, the screen **Create your first collection** has the same box.
2. Type the name in **Collection name (e.g. Cobrand)**. For example: `Partners`.
3. Read the line under the box: **Creates the folder** `…/partners`. The folder name comes from the name you typed, in lower case and with dashes.
4. Click **Create**.

</Steps>

The collection opens. It already has an environment called **local**:

<FileTree>

- my-workspace/
  - luvin.yaml `partners` is added to its `collections` list
  - partners/
    - collection.yaml
    - environments/
      - local.yaml

</FileTree>

If another collection already uses that folder name, Luvin adds a number: `partners-2`. The empty collection says **“Partners” is still empty**: add files next.

## Add files to a collection

Accepted files: `.yaml`, `.yml` and `.json`. They can be OpenAPI or AsyncAPI contracts, and Arazzo files. Luvin tells them apart by their content.

<Steps>

1. Open the collection.
2. Use one of these:
   - On **Home**, under **Quick actions**, click **Import Arazzo or contracts**. It appears when a collection is open.
   - In the sidebar, open the **⋯** menu of the collection and choose **Add files to the collection**.
   - At the bottom of the open collection in the sidebar, click **Add files to the collection**.
3. Pick the files in the dialog.

</Steps>

The workflows appear in the sidebar and the files are saved in the collection folder. Add the contracts the Arazzo files point to, or Luvin cannot find their operations.

### Drag and drop

You can drop files anywhere on the window. While you drag, an overlay says **Drop to add to «Partners»** and **OpenAPI or AsyncAPI contracts and Arazzo files (.yaml, .yml or .json); Postman or Bruno collections are imported on their own**. When you release, a note says **Added 3 file(s) to the collection**.

A Postman collection or environment, or a Bruno export, is not added as a file: it opens the [import dialog](#import-from-postman-or-bruno) with its summary. The same happens if you pick one with **Add files to the collection** or **Import contract…**. The other files of the same drop are added to the collection.

| Case | What you see |
|---|---|
| Some files are not `.yaml`, `.yml` or `.json` | **Added 2 file(s); 1 were not .yaml, .yml or .json**. The others are added. |
| None of the files is valid | **Only .yaml, .yml or .json files can be added** |
| No collection is open | The overlay says **Drop to import the collection (Luvin, Postman or Bruno)**. Postman and Bruno files open the import dialog, which creates its own collection. Other files show **Open or create a collection to add files to it**. |

## Import from Postman or Bruno

Each Postman or Bruno collection becomes a new Luvin collection, with its folders, requests, saved examples, authorization and environments.

<Steps>

1. Click **Import collection (Luvin, Postman or Bruno)**. It is under **Quick actions** on **Home**, and in the **⋯** menu of **Collections** in the sidebar.
2. For Postman, click **Choose .json files** and pick the collection export (v2.0 or v2.1). Pick the environment files too if you want their variables.
3. For Bruno, click **Choose folder** and pick the folder of the collection, or click **Choose .json**. You can also drop the files on the dialog.
4. Read the summary: requests, folders, examples and environments, plus warnings. Change the **Collection name** if you want.
5. Click **Import (12 requests)**.

</Steps>

![The Import collection dialog with a card for Luvin, one for Postman and one for Bruno](/media/en/import-postman.webp)

The same dialog imports a collection exported from Luvin (a `.luvin.json` file). See [Export and import a collection](/workspaces/export-import/).

Scripts come over as they were, in the **Scripts** tab of the collection, of each folder and of each request, and run once you trust the collection. An authorization or body type without an equivalent is left empty. The warnings in the dialog say which. The full walkthrough is in [Import](/get-started/import/) and [From Postman](/tutorials/from-postman/).

## Open a collection

Only one collection is open at a time.

- On **Home**, click its name. The open one has the badge **Open**.
- In the sidebar, click its name: the [collection overview](/workspaces/collection-overview/) opens and the collection expands.
- Click the arrow before the name to expand or collapse it. **Collapse all** in **Collection options** collapses everything.

There is no “close” for one collection inside a workspace. Open another collection, or close the workspace.

## Rename a collection

1. Open the **⋯** menu of the collection, on **Home** or in the sidebar, and choose **Rename**.
2. Type the new name and press Enter.

The folder on disk takes the new name too, so the workspace folder (and git) shows what the app shows. Renaming **test** to **Eloyfall cobrand** moves `test/` to `eloyfall-cobrand/`, with everything inside it.

| What moves with it | How |
|---|---|
| Its files: Arazzo files, contracts, environments, requests, mocks | The folder is moved as a whole. |
| `luvin.yaml` | Lists the new folder. |
| Secrets of its environments, requests and authorization | They are moved to the new folder on this computer. |
| History, the environment you chose and the downloaded contracts | Luvin remembers them for the new folder. |

- The folder name is the name in lowercase, without accents, with `-` instead of spaces. If another folder already has it, Luvin adds a number, such as `pagos-2`, and never overwrites a folder.
- If the new name gives the same folder, for example `Pagos` → `PAGOS`, only the visible name changes.
- A collection whose folder still has another name, because it was renamed with an older version, is fixed by confirming the same name again: open **Rename** and press Enter.
- The collection at the root of the workspace (folder `.`) does not move: only its visible name changes.
- In git, the change shows as files moved to the new folder. Commit it together, so your team gets the new folder.

<Aside type="caution">
Close other programs that are using files of the collection, such as an editor with one of its files open, before you rename it. On Windows an open file can stop the folder from moving; Luvin then shows the error and nothing changes.
</Aside>

## The ⋯ menu of a collection

![The ⋯ menu of a row in the sidebar](/media/en/sidebar-menu.webp)

The menu of a collection in the sidebar also creates things inside it:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Collection overview** | Opens the summary page. |
| **New request**, **Import from cURL**, **New folder** | Create requests and folders of requests. |
| **New Arazzo file**, **New OpenAPI contract**, **New AsyncAPI contract** | Create a file in the collection. |
| **Add files to the collection** | Adds files from your disk. |
| **Rename** | Changes the name and moves the folder to it. |
| **Show in Finder** (**Show in file explorer**) | Opens the folder in your file manager. |
| **Open in Terminal** | Opens a terminal in the folder. |
| **Remove from workspace** | Takes it out of the workspace. The files stay. |
| **Delete folder…** | Deletes the folder and its files. |

The menu on **Home** has **Open**, **Rename**, **Show in Finder**, **Remove from workspace** and **Delete folder…**.

## Remove or delete a collection

| | **Remove from workspace** | **Delete folder…** |
|---|---|---|
| Folder and files | Stay on disk. | Deleted. |
| `luvin.yaml` | The collection leaves the list. | The collection leaves the list. |
| Secrets of its environments | Stay on this computer. | Deleted. |
| Can you undo it? | Yes. Add the folder name to `collections` in `luvin.yaml`. | No. |

Both ask first. **Remove from workspace** asks **Remove “Partners” from the workspace? Its folder stays on disk and you can open it again.** Click **Remove**. **Delete folder…** asks **Delete “Partners” and its folder “partners”? Its Arazzo files, contracts, environments, cases and secrets are deleted. This can’t be undone.** Click **Delete**.

<Aside type="caution">
If you remove or delete the collection that is open, Luvin opens the first one that is left.
</Aside>

## Set the version

The collection version is a label your team chooses, such as `1.2.0`. It is not the version of an API.

<Steps>

1. Open the [collection overview](/workspaces/collection-overview/), on its **Overview** tab.
2. Under the name, click **Set version**. If there is one, click the version.
3. Type it, for example `1.2.0`, and click **Save** (or press Enter). Esc cancels.

</Steps>

It is saved as `collectionVersion` in `collection.yaml`. Empty text removes it. The generated documentation shows it.

## Copy the path and share

At the top of the [collection overview](/workspaces/collection-overview/), click **Copy path**. The button says **✓ Copied**. A collection is a folder of text files: send the path, or share it with [git](/workspaces/git/). Secrets are never in it.

## Order the collections

In the sidebar, open **Collection options** (the **⋯** next to **Collections**) and choose **Sort A-Z**. The order is saved in `luvin.yaml`.
