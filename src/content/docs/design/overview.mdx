A workflow is a chain of API calls where each step can use data from the previous ones. You build it in the **Design** tab, on a canvas where each step is a card, and Luvin writes the file for you in standard Arazzo.

<video controls muted playsinline preload="metadata" poster="/media/en/design-workflow.webp" src="/media/en/design-workflow.mp4"></video>

## Example: Pokemon card

**Pokemon card** has four steps. The first gets a Pokémon from the input `pokemon`. The other three use what it returned: its `id`, its main type and its first ability.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="In the app">

The canvas shows the green **▶ Start · 1 input**, then four cards, then **■ End of workflow**. The second card shows the parameter `idOrName` with the chip **← id · from 1**: its value is the `id` that step 1 gives.

![The Design tab: the floating toolbar, the canvas with full step cards and the step panel open over the right side](/media/en/builder.webp)

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="In the YAML">

```yaml title="pokemon.arazzo.yaml" {6,8,15}
- stepId: getPokemon
  operationId: $sourceDescriptions.pokeapi.getPokemon
  parameters:
    - name: idOrName
      in: path
      value: $inputs.pokemon
  outputs:
    id: $response.body#/id
    mainType: $response.body#/types/0/type/name
- stepId: getSpecies
  operationId: $sourceDescriptions.pokeapi.getPokemonSpecies
  parameters:
    - name: idOrName
      in: path
      value: $steps.getPokemon.outputs.id
```

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## What a workflow is made of

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Step** | One call: an operation of a contract, a saved request, a message or another workflow. |
| **Input** | A value the workflow asks for when it runs, for example the name of a Pokémon. |
| **Output** | A value a step takes from its response so the next steps can use it. |
| **Success criterion** | The rule that says a step went well, for example "it responds 200". |
| **Route** | What happens after a step: if it fails, or if it passes and a condition holds. It can retry, go to another step, continue in another workflow or end. |

A *contract* is the OpenAPI or AsyncAPI file that describes an API. The [glossary](/reference/glossary/) has every term.

## Open the Design tab

<Steps>
1. In the sidebar, click a workflow.
2. Click the **Design** tab.
</Steps>

The canvas shows the workflow from top to bottom. **Design** is the tab a workflow opens on, except in the Simple interface, which opens on **Overview**.

## The parts of the Design tab

The canvas fills the tab. Its tools float over it.

| Part | Where | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Toolbar | Top left | **↶ Undo**, **↷ Redo**, **Design** and **Run**, **＋ Step**, search, **⚠ to review**, **\{ \} YAML** and **⋯**. |
| **View** | Top right | What the canvas draws: the routes, the data lines and whether cards are full or minimized. |
| Canvas | Centre | **Start**, one card per step and **End of workflow**, from top to bottom. |
| Step panel | Over the right side | Opens when you click a card, with the tabs **Data**, **Success and routes**, **Authorization** and **Response**. |
| Zoom and minimap | Bottom | Zoom in and out, **⤢ Fit**, and the map of the whole workflow. |

Each card shows the full step: its parameters with where each value comes from, its success criteria, its outputs and its routes. A card can be minimized with **–**.

![A full step card: number and title, method and path, Parameters with their chips, Success criteria, Outputs and the On failure section in red](/media/en/design-card.webp)

[The canvas](/design/canvas/) explains each part, the window controls of the cards, the **View** options, the search and the keyboard shortcuts.
