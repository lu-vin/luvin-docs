Everything you build on the canvas is standard Arazzo. **\{ \} YAML** shows that text next to the canvas, so you see what each change writes without leaving the diagram.

## Example: see what a route writes

<Steps>
1. Open **Pokemon card** and click **Design**.
2. Click **\{ \} YAML** at the top left of the canvas. The file opens on the right, with line numbers.
3. Click the card of step 2. Its lines are marked and scroll into view.
4. Add a route to step 2, for example **Retry** if it responds 429. See [Add or change a route](/design/branch-menu/).
</Steps>

The `onFailure` of step 2 appears in the YAML as soon as you click **Add**.

![The YAML of pokemon.arazzo.yaml next to the canvas, with the lines of step 2 marked](/media/en/design-yaml-peek.webp)

## What the panel shows

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| Header | The name of the file, the Arazzo version and **updates with the canvas**. **×** closes it. |
| Body | The whole file, with line numbers. The lines of the selected step are marked. |
| Foot | **Select a step and its lines are marked; click a line to select its step.** and **Edit in YAML** |

It is the same text the canvas writes, not a copy: every change on the canvas shows here at once.

## Go from the canvas to the YAML and back

- **Select a step** on the canvas: its lines are marked and scroll into view.
- **Select Start**: the rules for every step (`successActions` and `failureActions`) are marked.
- **Click a line** inside a step: that step is selected on the canvas.

## Edit the text

The panel is for reading. To edit, click **Edit in YAML**: the [YAML tab](/design/arazzo-editor/) opens on the same file, with validation and autocompletion. The canvas draws the changes when you come back.

## Open and close it

- **\{ \} YAML** opens and closes it. While a panel is open on the right, including this one, the button shows only **\{ \}**.
- **Esc** or **×** closes it.

The YAML takes the place of the step panel: while it is open, clicking a card marks its lines instead of opening its panel. Close the YAML to see the panel again. Drag its left edge to make it wider or narrower.
