A reusable is an error route or a parameter that you define once and use in several steps. Change it in one place and every step that uses it changes. Use reusables for the things that repeat: "on a 401, end the workflow", or a header every call needs.

In the Arazzo file, reusables live in a section called `components`. The PayPal example uses them. Its error actions `unauthorized` (end the workflow on a 401) and `notApproved` (end on a 422) are used by several steps. Its parameters `requestId` (the `PayPal-Request-Id` header) and `fullResponse` (the `Prefer` header) are used by the steps that create or capture an order.

<Aside type="note">
Reusables belong to the file, not to one workflow. A reusable saved in one workflow can be applied in any other workflow of the same file.
</Aside>

## Example: PayPal

The PayPal file defines its reusables once, in `components`. **Create order** then applies them with `reference`.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="In the app">

On the card of `createOrder`, the parameters read ♻ `PayPal-Request-Id` and ♻ `Prefer`, and the routes under **On failure** start with ♻. In the step panel, the **Data** tab shows the two headers with a ♻ badge and **Detach**. The **Success and routes** tab shows the routes for **400** and **401** with ♻ `invalidRequest` and ♻ `unauthorized`.

![The Data tab of createOrder in the PayPal example: its headers use the reusables PayPal-Request-Id and Prefer, marked with ♻](/media/en/reusables.webp)

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="In the YAML">

```yaml title="paypal.arazzo.yaml" {2-5,8,12-13}
components:
  failureActions:
    unauthorized:
      name: unauthorized
      type: end
      criteria:
        - condition: $statusCode == 401
# ...
- stepId: createOrder
  parameters:
    - reference: $components.parameters.requestId
    - reference: $components.parameters.fullResponse
  onFailure:
    - reference: $components.failureActions.invalidRequest
    - reference: $components.failureActions.unauthorized
```

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Where to find them

The reusables of the file are listed in the panel of **Start**.

<Steps>
1. On the canvas, click **Start**, the green button at the top.
2. Go to **Reusables**, at the end of the panel. The section only appears when the file has at least one.
</Steps>

The section says **Defined once in the file. Click to edit them, or drag them onto a step to apply them.** Each row shows the name, what it does and how many steps use it, for example **3 uses**.

![The Start panel of Create order in the PayPal example: the Reusables section with the error routes and the parameters of the file](/media/en/design-reusables-start.webp)

## Save an error route as a reusable

You need a step with a route for one status, for example "if it responds 404, end the workflow". See [Add or change a route](/design/branch-menu/).

<Steps>
1. In the **Design** tab, click the step.
2. Open the **Success and routes** tab.
3. Under **Routes**, find the route for that status and click **Save as reusable**.
4. Type a name. The box suggests one, for example `on404`.
5. Click **Save**.
</Steps>

The step keeps working as before. The route now shows a ♻ badge with the name and a **Detach** link. If the name is already taken, Luvin adds a number (`on4042`). Letters, numbers, `-` and `_` are kept; anything else is removed.

Only "if it fails" routes for a single status can be saved this way.

## Save a parameter as a reusable

<Steps>
1. Click the step and stay on the **Data** tab.
2. Under **Receives**, find a parameter that has a value, for example a fixed header.
3. Click ♻ (**Save as reusable**) on its row.
4. Type a name and click **Save**. **Cancel** closes the box.
</Steps>

The button does not appear on a parameter without a value, or on a workflow input of a step that calls another workflow. The row now shows a ♻ badge with the name of the parameter and **Detach**.

## Apply a reusable in another step

| How | What happens |
|---|---|
| Drag its row from the **Start** panel onto a step card | The reusable is added to that step. |
| Open the reusable, then click a step on the canvas and click **Apply to “step”** | The same, on that step. Without a step selected, the panel says **Pick a step on the canvas to apply it (or drag it onto the card).** |
| In the route editor of a step, under **Or apply a reusable:**, click ♻ name | The step uses the reusable for that status. The editor only offers reusables that handle the status you typed. See [Add or change a route](/design/branch-menu/). |

## Edit a reusable

<Steps>
1. Click its row in the **Start** panel, or the ♻ badge of a parameter in the **Data** tab of a step.
2. Change it in the panel. At the top there is a tag, ♻ **error action** or ♻ **parameter**, and the name of the reusable, which you can edit.
3. To go back to the step, click **← Back to the step**.
</Steps>

| Reusable | What you can change |
|---|---|
| Error action | **If it responds** (the status code) and **Then**: **End the workflow**, **Retry (up to 3 times)** or **Go to “step”**. With retry, two more fields: `retryLimit` and `retryAfter (s)`. |
| Parameter | **Parameter name**, `in` (path, query, header or cookie) and **Value**. |

Every change applies to all the steps that use it. The section **Used in** lists them, with their workflow. If you rename the reusable, Luvin updates every step that uses it.

![The panel of the reusable unauthorized: error action, If it responds 401, Then End the workflow, and Used in with five steps](/media/en/design-reusable-panel.webp)

## Detach it from a step

Detach when one step needs a different behaviour and the others do not.

<Steps>
1. Click the step.
2. Click **Detach** next to the ♻ badge: in the **Data** tab for a parameter, or under **Routes** in **Success and routes** for an error route.
</Steps>

The step keeps its own copy of the route or parameter, and the reusable stays in the file. A route that was named `unauthorized` goes back to the name of its status, for example `on401`.

## Delete a reusable

<Steps>
1. Click the reusable in the **Start** panel to open it.
2. Click the bin at the top.
</Steps>

The bin is off while any step uses it. Its tooltip says **Used in 2 step(s): remove it from there first**. Detach it from each step first. When no step uses it, the bin deletes it from the file.

<Aside type="tip">
If you delete a reusable by mistake, click ↶ (**Undo**) in the toolbar of the canvas. See [Organize a workflow](/design/organize/).
</Aside>
