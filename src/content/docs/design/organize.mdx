Once a workflow has steps, you will want to move some, rename others and remove the ones you do not need. This page shows how, and what Luvin does with the data that other steps took from them.

## Example: move a step above the one it reads

In **Pokemon card**, `getSpecies` reads `$steps.getPokemon.outputs.id`. Drag `getSpecies` above `getPokemon`.

<Steps>
1. Drag the `getSpecies` card up and drop it above `getPokemon`.
2. A bar appears: **Check the order:** **“getSpecies” uses data from “getPokemon”, which now runs after it**.
3. Click **Undo** in the bar. The steps go back to their places.
</Steps>

## Reorder steps

Steps run from top to bottom, in the order they appear. There are three ways to move one.

| How | Steps |
|---|---|
| Drag | Drag the card up or down on the canvas and drop it between two other cards. |
| Arrows on the card | Point at the card and click **↑** or **↓**. |
| Arrows in the panel | Click the step, then **Move up** or **Move down** at the top of its panel. |

The arrow that would move the first step up, or the last step down, is off.

Only the vertical position counts. Moving a card sideways changes the drawing, not the order, and **Reset layout** puts the drawing back.

### When the new order breaks a connection

A step can only use data from steps that run before it. If you move a step above one it takes data from, a bar appears: **Check the order:** and the detail, for example **“getBenefits” uses data from “getCards”, which now runs after it**. Luvin does not change the connections. Click **Undo** in the bar, or move the other step too. Undoing the move, from the bar, from **Undo** above the canvas or with ⌘ + Z, also takes the bar away. The [YAML tab](/design/arazzo-editor/) and the step's panel also show the error **Uses an output of “getCards”, which runs later.**

![The bar Check the order: “getOrder” uses data from “createOrder”, which now runs after it, with Undo and Close](/media/en/design-order-warning.webp)

**Undo** in the bar puts the steps back; **Close** hides the bar.

## Rename a step

<Steps>
1. Click the step.
2. Click its name at the top of the panel.
3. Type the new name and press Enter, or click outside.
</Steps>

A name can have letters, numbers, `-` and `_`, without spaces, and it cannot repeat in the workflow. If it does not fit these rules, Luvin shows an error above the canvas and keeps the old name.

Renaming is safe. Luvin updates every place that points to the step: the values that read its outputs (`$steps.name.outputs…`), the actions that jump to it and the `dependsOn` of other steps.

To rename the whole workflow, see [Create a workflow](/design/create/).

## Delete a step

<Steps>
1. Click the step.
2. Press Delete or Backspace, or click the bin (**Remove the step**) at the top of its panel.
</Steps>

The step disappears from the canvas and from the file.

Other steps may have used its data. In that case a red bar above the canvas names them: **Other steps used what you removed and lost that data: createOrder.** Luvin keeps their references, which now point to nothing. Each of those steps shows **1 error(s)** on its card, and its panel shows an error such as **Uses $steps.getPokemon.outputs.id, but step "getPokemon" does not exist.** Give those values another source (see [Connect data between steps](/design/wire-data/)), or click **Undo** to bring the step back.

![After deleting listProducts: the red bar names createOrder, and its card has a red border and says 1 error(s), because it read the sku of the deleted step](/media/en/design-dangling.webp)

## Keyboard and right-click on the canvas

| On | Do | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| A data line | Click the line or its label, then press **Delete** or **Backspace** | The connection is removed: the parameter is left without a source. The line is highlighted while selected. |
| A data line | Right-click | **Remove this connection** |
| An arrow of the order | Right-click | **Insert a step here** |
| A step | Right-click | **See the step details**, **Insert a step after**, **Move up**, **Move down**, **Delete the step** |
| The background | Right-click | **Add a step at the end**, **Fit to view**, and show or hide the actions by response and the data flow |

**Undo** brings back anything you removed this way.

## Undo and redo

Above the canvas, **Undo** and **Redo** step back and forward through the changes you made in the **Design** tab: adding, moving, renaming and deleting steps, connections, values and outputs.

| Action | macOS | Windows |
|---|---|---|
| **Undo** | ⌘ + Z | Ctrl + Z |
| **Redo** | ⇧ + ⌘ + Z | Ctrl + Shift + Z |

The shortcuts do not work while the cursor is in a text field. Luvin remembers up to the last 100 changes. A button is off when there is nothing to undo or redo.

## Delete a workflow

A workflow is deleted from the sidebar, with everything that hangs from it: its steps, its inputs, its cases and its results.

![The menu of a workflow row in the sidebar, with the action Delete workflow…](/media/en/sidebar-menu.webp)

<Steps>
1. In the sidebar, point at the workflow row and click **⋯**.
2. Choose **Delete workflow…**. A confirmation appears under the row.
3. Click **Delete**, or **Cancel** to keep it.
</Steps>

The confirmation says **Delete the workflow “Type explorer” with its steps, inputs and cases?** If other workflows of the file use it, it adds who: **Used by: Battle matchup. They will show errors until you adjust them.** Luvin removes the workflow from the `dependsOn` of the others. A step that calls it, or a value that reads its outputs, stays as it was and shows an error until you fix it. 

### The last workflow of a file

An Arazzo file needs at least one workflow, so the last one cannot be deleted alone. What you see depends on where you work:

| Where | What the confirmation says | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| In a workspace | **“Name” is the only workflow in file.arazzo.yaml: the file and its cases will be deleted. This can’t be undone.** | **Delete** deletes the whole file. The contracts stay. |
| Without a workspace | **“Name” is the only workflow in file.arazzo.yaml, and an Arazzo needs at least one.** | Only **Close**. Add another workflow first. |

<Aside type="caution">
Deleting a workflow or a file is not part of **Undo**. Read the name in the confirmation before you click **Delete**.
</Aside>
