Once a workflow has steps, you will want to move some, rename others and remove the ones you do not need. This page shows how, and what Luvin does with the data that other steps took from them.

## Example: move a step above the one it reads

In **Pokemon card**, step 2 `getSpecies` reads the `id` of step 1, `getPokemon`: its chip says **← id · from 1**.

<Steps>
1. Drag the `getSpecies` card up and drop it above `getPokemon`.
2. A bar appears: **Check the order:** **“getSpecies” uses data from “getPokemon”, which now runs after it**.
3. Click **Undo** in the bar. The steps go back to their places.
</Steps>

## Reorder steps

Steps run from top to bottom, in the order they appear. There are four ways to move one.

| How | Steps |
|---|---|
| Drag | Drag the card up or down on the canvas and drop it between two other cards. |
| Keyboard | Select the step and press **⌥↑** or **⌥↓** (Alt+↑ or Alt+↓). |
| Right-click | Right-click the card and choose **Move up** or **Move down**. |
| The panel | Click the step, then **⋯** at the bottom of its panel, and **Move up** or **Move down**. |

**Move up** is off on the first step, and **Move down** on the last one.

Only the vertical position counts. Moving a card sideways changes the drawing, not the order, and **Reset layout**, in **View**, puts the drawing back.

### When the new order breaks a connection

A step can only use data from steps that run before it. If you move a step above one it takes data from, a bar appears: **Check the order:** and the detail, for example **“getBenefits” uses data from “getCards”, which now runs after it**. Luvin does not change the connections. Click **Undo** in the bar, or move the other step too. Undoing the move, from the bar, from **↶** above the canvas or with ⌘ + Z, also takes the bar away. The [YAML tab](/design/arazzo-editor/) and the step's panel also show the error **Uses an output of “getCards”, which runs later.**

![The bar Check the order: “getOrder” uses data from “createOrder”, which now runs after it, with Undo and Close](/media/en/design-order-warning.webp)

**Undo** in the bar puts the steps back; **Close** hides the bar.

## Give a step a title and an id

A step has a title, which is what the card shows, and an id, which is what expressions use to read its outputs (`$steps.getPokemon.outputs.id`).

### Change the title

<Steps>
1. Click the step.
2. Click the title at the top of its panel. Without one, it shows the summary of the operation in grey.
3. Type a short sentence, for example "Get the Pokémon".
</Steps>

The title is saved as the `description` of the step, as you type.

### Change the id

<Steps>
1. Click the step.
2. Under the title, click the id with **✎**. Its tooltip says **Change the step id (expressions use it; it is updated across the Arazzo)**.
3. Type the new id and press Enter, or click outside.
</Steps>

An id can have letters, numbers, `-` and `_`, without spaces, and it cannot repeat in the workflow. If it does not fit these rules, Luvin shows an error above the canvas and keeps the old id.

Changing the id is safe. Luvin updates every place that points to the step: the values that read its outputs (`$steps.id.outputs…`), the routes that go to it and the `dependsOn` of other steps.

To rename the whole workflow, see [Create a workflow](/design/create/).

## Remove a step

<Steps>
1. Start the removal in one of these ways:
   - click **×** (**Remove the step**) in the header of the card;
   - select the step and press **⌫** (Backspace) or Delete;
   - right-click the card and choose **Delete the step**, or use **⋯** at the bottom of its panel.
2. A window asks **Remove step 2?** and lists **This breaks in the workflow**: the steps that use its data, the routes that go to it, the steps that depend on it and the outputs of the workflow that come from it. If nothing breaks, it says **No other step uses its data or has routes to it.**
3. Click **Remove the step**, or **Cancel**.
</Steps>

![The window Remove step 2? with This breaks in the workflow: step 3 uses its data, and the Cancel and Remove the step buttons](/media/en/design-remove-confirm.webp)

**Cancel** has the focus, so pressing Enter does not remove anything. With several steps selected, the window says **Remove 3 steps?** and the button **Remove 3 steps**.

The step disappears from the canvas and from the file.

If other steps used its data, a red bar above the canvas names them: **Other steps used what you removed and lost that data: createOrder.** Luvin keeps their references, which now point to nothing. Their chips turn red and say **missing**, the card shows **1 error(s)**, and the panel shows an error such as **Uses $steps.getPokemon.outputs.id, but step "getPokemon" does not exist.** Give those values another source (see [Connect data between steps](/design/wire-data/)), or undo to bring the step back.

![After removing listProducts: the red bar names createOrder, and its card has a red border and says 1 error(s), because it read the sku of the removed step](/media/en/design-dangling.webp)

## Right-click on the canvas

| On | What it offers |
|---|---|
| A data line | **Remove this connection**. You can also click the line and press Delete or Backspace. |
| An arrow of the order | **Insert a step here** |
| A step | **See the step details**, **Copy**, **Paste after this step** (when you copied steps), **Duplicate**, **Add to the selection**, **Extract to a workflow…**, the run options, **Add route…**, **Insert a step after**, **Move up**, **Move down** and **Delete the step** |
| The background | **Add a step at the end**, **Fit to view**, **Hide actions by response** (or **Show…**) and the three options of **Data** |

The run options are explained in [Debug a workflow](/test/debug/). **Copy**, **Duplicate** and **Extract** are in [Copy, duplicate and extract steps](/design/copy-and-extract/), and **Add route…** in [Add or change a route](/design/branch-menu/).

Undo brings back anything you removed this way.

## Undo and redo

Above the canvas, **↶** (**Undo**) and **↷** (**Redo**) step back and forward through the changes you made in the **Design** tab: adding, moving, renaming and removing steps, connections, values, outputs and routes.

| Action | macOS | Windows |
|---|---|---|
| **Undo** | ⌘ + Z | Ctrl + Z |
| **Redo** | ⇧ + ⌘ + Z | Ctrl + Shift + Z |

The shortcuts do not work while the cursor is in a text field. Luvin remembers up to the last 100 changes. A button is off when there is nothing to undo or redo.

## Delete a workflow

A workflow is deleted from the sidebar, with everything that hangs from it: its steps, its inputs, its cases and its results.

![The menu of a workflow row in the sidebar, with the action Delete workflow…](/media/en/sidebar-menu.webp)

<Steps>
1. In the sidebar, point at the workflow row and click **⋯**.
2. Choose **Delete workflow…**. A confirmation appears under the row.
3. Click **Delete**, or **Cancel** to keep it.
</Steps>

The confirmation says **Delete the workflow “Type explorer” with its steps, inputs and cases?** If other workflows of the file use it, it adds who: **Used by: Battle matchup. They will show errors until you adjust them.** Luvin removes the workflow from the `dependsOn` of the others. A step that calls it, or a value that reads its outputs, stays as it was and shows an error until you fix it. 

### The last workflow of a file

An Arazzo file needs at least one workflow, so the last one cannot be deleted alone. What you see depends on where you work:

| Where | What the confirmation says | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| In a workspace | **“Name” is the only workflow in file.arazzo.yaml: the file and its cases will be deleted. This can’t be undone.** | **Delete** deletes the whole file. The contracts stay. |
| Without a workspace | **“Name” is the only workflow in file.arazzo.yaml, and an Arazzo needs at least one.** | Only **Close**. Add another workflow first. |

<Aside type="caution">
Deleting a workflow or a file is not part of **Undo**. Read the name in the confirmation before you click **Delete**.
</Aside>
