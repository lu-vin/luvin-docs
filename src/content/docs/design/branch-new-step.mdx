Sometimes a response needs its own call. When the species of a Pokémon is not found, you may want to list Pokémon; when an order is rejected, you may want to read why. **or a new step…**, in the route editor, adds that step and connects it to the route in one go, without touching the steps of the happy path.

<video controls muted playsinline preload="metadata" poster="/media/en/design-branch.webp" src="/media/en/design-branch.mp4"></video>

## Example: list Pokémon when the species is not found

<Steps>
1. Open **Pokemon card** and click the **Design** tab.
2. On the card of `getSpecies`, click **＋ What to do if it fails**. If the contract declares the 404 and nothing handles it yet, you can also click **Choose what to do** on its red row.
3. In **If it responds**, type `404` or click it next to **the contract says:**.
4. Click **Go to another step**.
5. Next to **Go to step**, click **or a new step…**. The editor closes and a search opens, titled **New step if it responds 404**.
6. Click the row `GET /pokemon`. The whole row is clickable.
</Steps>

The search closes. What you see:

- A new card at the bottom, `listPokemon`, selected and with its panel open.
- On the card of `getSpecies`, the row `404 → goes to step 5 · …`, and a red dashed arrow that goes down the left side to `listPokemon`.
- On the card of `getAbility`, a new row in **If it passes and matches**: `success → ends successfully (End)`, with a green arrow to **End of workflow**.

![The canvas after adding the step: getSpecies has the 404 route to the new card at the bottom, with a red dashed arrow down the left side, and the last step of the happy path has the success route that ends the workflow, with a green arrow to End of workflow](/media/en/design-branch-done.webp)

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Before">

```yaml title="pokemon.arazzo.yaml"
steps:
  - stepId: getPokemon
    # …
  - stepId: getSpecies
    # …
    outputs:
      # …
  - stepId: getWeakness
    # …
  - stepId: getAbility
    # …
    outputs:
      # …
```

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="After">

```yaml title="pokemon.arazzo.yaml" {8-13,20-22,23-27}
steps:
  - stepId: getPokemon
    # …
  - stepId: getSpecies
    # …
    outputs:
      # …
    onFailure:
      - name: on404
        type: goto
        stepId: listPokemon
        criteria:
          - condition: $statusCode == 404
  - stepId: getWeakness
    # …
  - stepId: getAbility
    # …
    outputs:
      # …
    onSuccess:
      - name: done
        type: end
  - stepId: listPokemon
    description: Lista Pokémon (paginado)
    operationId: $sourceDescriptions.pokeapi.listPokemon
    successCriteria:
      - condition: $statusCode == 200
```

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The description of `listPokemon` is the summary of the operation in the contract of the example.

## The search

It is the same search as **＋ Step**, with a line of help: *Choose the operation, request or workflow. It goes at the end of the workflow, wired to this branch; the happy path ends before reaching it.*

![The search New step if it responds 404 with its help line, Search operation… and the operations of the contracts of the collection](/media/en/design-branch-pick.webp)

| Part | What it has |
|---|---|
| **Search operation…** | Filters every group by path, operation id, summary or tag. |
| **Suggested · use data you already have** | Operations that take a value an earlier step already gives. Only before you type. |
| **Collection requests** | Your saved HTTP requests, when the collection has any. |
| **Workflows in this Arazzo** | The other workflows of the file, called as one step. |
| **From other Arazzo files** | Workflows of other Arazzo files of the collection. |
| One group per contract | Its operations. |

**Patterns · standard Arazzo** is not offered here: a pattern is added with **＋ Step**. See [Standard Arazzo patterns](/design/patterns/).

How to pick:

- Click anywhere on a row, or the **+** at its end. Its tooltip says **New step if it responds 404**.
- Or move with ↑ ↓ and press Enter. Enter in the search box adds the first row that matches.

To close it without adding anything, press **Esc** or click outside it. Nothing changes.

## What Luvin does, in order

<Steps>
1. **Adds the step at the end of the workflow**, after the step that is last now. Luvin fills it in by the same rules as any new step: name, description, success criterion, required parameters and body. See [What a new step gets automatically](/design/add-steps/#what-a-new-step-gets-automatically).
2. **Connects the route.** The step that got the response gets a route `on404` of type `goto` that points to the new step. If it already had a route for 404, Luvin changes that one, in its place, and keeps its name.
3. **Ends the happy path before the new step.** The step right above the new one gets a route on success named `done`, of type `end`.
4. **Selects the new step** and opens its panel, so you can check its data.
</Steps>

If you picked a saved request, its card may show **required not marked**: say which fields the API requires when you want. See [Mark the required fields of a request](/design/required-fields/).

## Why `done`

In Arazzo, after a step passes, the run goes on with the next step of the list. The new step is now the next one after `getAbility`. Without `done`, a run where everything goes well would go from `getAbility` into `listPokemon`, a step meant only for the 404.

| Run | Without `done` | With `done` |
|---|---|---|
| Everything goes well | `getPokemon` → `getSpecies` → `getWeakness` → `getAbility` → `listPokemon` | `getPokemon` → `getSpecies` → `getWeakness` → `getAbility`, then ends |
| `getSpecies` responds 404 | `getPokemon` → `getSpecies` → `listPokemon` | The same |

In a run that goes well, `listPokemon` shows **Not run: the workflow ended earlier due to an end/goto action.** That is expected.

When the run takes the route, `getSpecies` shows its 404 and **Goes to step "listPokemon" (action "on404")**. `getWeakness` and `getAbility` do not run. After `listPokemon`, the workflow ends, because it is the last step.

### When Luvin does not add `done`

Luvin leaves the step above as it is when it already leaves on success: when it has a route on success of type `end` or `goto` without conditions. For example, a step written like this keeps its own route and gets no `done`:

```yaml
onSuccess:
  - name: finished
    type: end
```

## Several steps for routes

Each new step for a route goes at the end too, so the step that was last before it, which is the previous route step, gets its own `done`.

Continuing the example, send the 404 of `getAbility` to `GET /pokemon` as well. Luvin names the new step `listPokemon2`, because `listPokemon` is taken:

```yaml title="pokemon.arazzo.yaml" {6-11,14-16}
  - stepId: getAbility
    # …
    onSuccess:
      - name: done
        type: end
    onFailure:
      - name: on404
        type: goto
        stepId: listPokemon2
        criteria:
          - condition: $statusCode == 404
  - stepId: listPokemon
    # …
    onSuccess:
      - name: done
        type: end
  - stepId: listPokemon2
    # …
```

Each of these steps ends the workflow when it passes, so one never runs into the next.

## Check the new step

<Steps>
1. Look at the panel of the new step. Each required parameter has a source.
2. Check where each value comes from. Luvin may take data from any step above the new one, including steps the route skips. In the example, `getWeakness` and `getAbility` never run when `getSpecies` answers 404, so their outputs are empty on that path.
3. If a value comes from a skipped step, its chip turns amber with ⚠, and [To review](/design/review/) says which route skips it. Change its source to a step that runs before the route, or to an input. See [Connect data between steps](/design/wire-data/).
4. Open the **Cases** tab. The generated case **getSpecies responds 404 → on404** simulates the 404 and checks that the run goes to `listPokemon`. To follow the path on the canvas without calling the API, use **▷ Probe** on the route: see [Probe routes and coverage](/test/probe-routes/).
</Steps>

## Undo

| What you picked | How to undo it all |
|---|---|
| An operation or a workflow | **Undo** once, or ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z). The step, the route and `done` go away together. |
| A saved request | **Undo** twice. The first one removes the route and `done`, the second one the step. |

**Redo**, or ⇧⌘Z (Ctrl+Shift+Z), puts them back.

### Remove a route to a new step

Later, when Undo is no longer an option, remove the parts yourself:

<Steps>
1. Click the pencil of the row `404 → goes to step 5 · …` and click **Remove the route**. Or change it to another option.
2. Remove the step with **×** on its card. The confirmation lists what breaks; click **Remove the step**.
3. Remove the `done` route of the step above, in the **Success and routes** tab of its panel, if you want the happy path to go on to a step you add later.
</Steps>

If you remove the step first, the confirmation warns **A route of step 2 «…» goes to this step: it will break.** If you remove it anyway, the row says **goes to a step that no longer exists (listPokemon)** and [To review](/design/review/) lists it.

## Add more steps after a route step

The steps below the happy path belong to the routes. Keep that in mind when you add more:

| How you add it | Where it goes | Runs on the happy path? |
|---|---|---|
| The **+** on the arrow right below the step with `done` | Between them. `done` moves to the new step. | Yes |
| **＋ Step** with no step selected | After the step with `done`. `done` moves to the new step. | Yes |
| **＋ Step** with the step with `done` selected, or dropped right below it | After it. `done` moves to the new step. | Yes |
| **＋ Step** with a route step selected, or dropped below a route step | After that route step | No: it runs after that route step |

See [Insert a step](/design/insert-step/).

<Aside type="caution">
Moving a step, with ⌥↑ ⌥↓, **Move up**, **Move down** or by dragging its card, does not move `done`. If you move a route step above the end of the happy path, check the routes on success of each step.
</Aside>
