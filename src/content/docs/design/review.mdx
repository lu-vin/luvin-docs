Some mistakes pass every check of the Arazzo schema and only show when the workflow runs: a route that skips the step that gives a value, a route to a step that no longer exists, a condition that can never hold. **To review** reads the Arazzo the way the runner would and points them out on the canvas, with what to do.

The warnings never block: you can still run the workflow.

## Example: a JSONPath condition that is really a comparison

In a workflow on the PokéAPI, `getSpecies` checks the capture rate with a JSONPath criterion:

```yaml title="pokemon.arazzo.yaml" {4-6}
- stepId: getSpecies
  successCriteria:
    - condition: $statusCode == 200
    - context: $response.body
      condition: $.capture_rate >= 0
      type: jsonpath
```

The schema accepts it. But `$.capture_rate >= 0` is not a JSONPath query: it finds nothing, so the criterion never holds and the step always fails, whatever the API answers.

<Steps>
1. Above the canvas, the toolbar shows the amber chip **⚠ 1 to review**.
2. Click it. The menu **To review · Luvin reads the Arazzo the way it would run it** lists: **Step 2: the criterion «$.capture_rate >= 0» is not valid JSONPath and is never met: the step always fails.** Below it: **Write it as a simple condition: «$response.body#/capture_rate >= 0». Any Arazzo tool evaluates it that way.**
3. Click the warning. Luvin selects `getSpecies` and opens its **Success and routes** tab, where the same warning has the button **Change it**.
4. Click **Change it**.
</Steps>

![The menu of the chip ⚠ 1 to review · 3 without a route: the JSONPath criterion of step 2 that is never met, and the statuses without a route](/media/en/design-review-menu.webp)

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="After">

```yaml title="pokemon.arazzo.yaml" {4}
- stepId: getSpecies
  successCriteria:
    - condition: $statusCode == 200
    - condition: $response.body#/capture_rate >= 0
```

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The warning goes away. ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z) undoes the change.

![The Success and routes tab of getSpecies: the box To review with the button Change it, and the criterion marked with ⚠](/media/en/design-review-fix.webp)

## The chip and its menu

The chip is in the toolbar of the canvas, in amber, when there is something to review. It counts two things:

- **N to review**: problems that change what the run does.
- **N without a route**: statuses the contract declares that nothing handles. These are informative.

For example, **⚠ 1 to review · 4 without a route**. With the step panel open, the chip shows only the total; hover over it to read both numbers.

Click the chip to open the list. Each warning says what happens and, below, what to do. Click one to go there: Luvin selects the step and brings it into view, and opens the tab where you fix it. **Data** for a value, **Success and routes** for a route or a condition. A warning about the outputs of the workflow selects **End of workflow**.

The chip is hidden while the canvas shows the results of a run. See [Results on the canvas](/test/run-on-canvas/).

## What it finds

| Warning | Level | Where else you see it |
|---|---|---|
| [A value that a route can skip](#a-value-that-a-route-can-skip) | Warning | The data chip turns amber with ⚠; a box in the **Data** tab of the step, or in the panel of **End of workflow**. |
| [A route to a step that no longer exists](#a-route-to-a-step-that-no-longer-exists) | Error | ⚠ on the route row. |
| [A loop only the runner stops](#a-loop-only-the-runner-stops) | Warning | ⚠ on the route row. |
| [A status without a route](#a-status-without-a-route) | Information | The red row `✕ no route: the workflow fails` on the card. |
| [A JSONPath condition that never holds](#a-jsonpath-condition-that-never-holds) | Error | ⚠ next to the criterion or on the route row; a box in **Success and routes** with **Change it**. |

Hover over a ⚠ to read the warning and what to do.

### A value that a route can skip

A route that jumps forward skips the steps in between. If a later step uses a value one of them gives, that value arrives empty when the route is taken.

> **Step 4: «orderId» comes from step 3, which the route «if it responds 409» of step 2 skips.**
> If that route is taken, it arrives here with no value. Change the route so it does not skip step 3, or take the data from another step.

On the card, the chip of that value turns amber, for example `← orderId · from 3 ⚠`.

The outputs of the workflow are checked too, in the panel of **End of workflow**. In **Pokemon card**, with a route that sends the 404 of step 2 to a step at the bottom:

- **End: the output «weakTo» comes from step 3, which the route «if it responds 404» of step 2 skips.**
- **End: the output «weakTo» comes from step 3, and the route «if it passes» of step 2 ends earlier.**, when a route ends the workflow successfully before the step that gives it.

What to do: **If that route is taken, the workflow returns that output empty. Change the route or remove the output.** A parameter of a step has no default value in Arazzo, so the fix is in the route or in the source of the value. See [Connect data between steps](/design/wire-data/).

### A route to a step that no longer exists

> **Step 2: the route «if it responds 404» goes to step «listPokemon», which no longer exists.**
> Pick another step in the route, or remove it.

It happens when you remove or rename the target outside the canvas, for example in the YAML tab. The row of the route says **goes to a step that no longer exists (listPokemon)**. Click its pencil to choose another step, or remove it. See [Add or change a route](/design/branch-menu/).

### A loop only the runner stops

A route that goes back to an earlier step, or to the same one, has no limit in Arazzo. Luvin stops a run after 200 executions. When the route depends only on the status, the API can answer the same every time:

> **Step 3: the route «if it responds 401» goes back to step 1 with no limit. If the API keeps responding the same, the runner cuts it at 200 executions and the workflow fails.**
> Use «Retry» or «Renew and retry», which have an attempt limit, or add a condition on the response that stops holding.

A route on success with no condition at all always goes back: **Step 3: the route «if it passes» always goes back to step 1. Nothing stops it before the runner's 200 executions, and the workflow fails.**

A loop that checks a field of the response is not flagged. For example, a pagination that repeats the step while `$response.body#/next != null` stops by itself on the last page. See [Standard Arazzo patterns](/design/patterns/).

### A status without a route

> **Step 2: the API can respond 404 and there is no route: the workflow fails.**
> Choose what to do with 404, or leave it if that failure is expected.

These are the red rows of the card. You do not have to handle them all: they are there so you decide. See [A route for any status](/design/branch-other-status/#declared-statuses-without-a-route-one-click).

### A JSONPath condition that never holds

A criterion of type `jsonpath` written as a comparison, such as `$.legendary == true` or `$.capture_rate >= 0`, is not a valid JSONPath query. The runner finds nothing, so:

| Where it is | What happens | Warning |
|---|---|---|
| Success criteria of a step | The step always fails. | **Step 1: the criterion «$.legendary == true» is not valid JSONPath and is never met: the step always fails.** |
| A route of a step | The route is never taken. | **Step 1: the condition «$.legendary == true» of the route «ifLegendary» is not valid JSONPath and is never met: the route is never taken.** |
| A rule of the workflow | The rule never applies. | **Start: the condition «$.legendary == true» of the rule «…» is not valid JSONPath and is never met: the rule never applies.** The ⚠ goes on its row in the panel of **Start**. |

No Arazzo tool will match these conditions either, so Luvin does not run them as comparisons: it proposes the standard form.

When the `context` is `$response.body` or `$message.payload`, **Change it** rewrites only that condition as a simple condition, `$response.body#/legendary == true`, and removes its `context` and `type`. The rest of the YAML, comments included, stays as it was.

With any other `context`, there is no one-click fix. The warning says what to do: **Write it as a simple condition on the body («$response.body#/field == value»), or as a JSONPath query that finds something.**

Valid JSONPath queries are not flagged. `$.abilities[?(@.is_hidden == false)]` holds when it finds at least one ability that is not hidden. See [Check more than the status](/recipes/criteria/).

<Aside type="tip">
After **Change it**, **▷ Probe** can also simulate the route: it answers with that value in the body and follows the path. See [Probe routes and coverage](/test/probe-routes/).
</Aside>
