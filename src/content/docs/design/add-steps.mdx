A step is one call in the workflow. You add steps with **＋ Step**, at the top left of the canvas. It opens a search with everything you can add: the operations of your contracts, the requests of the collection, other workflows and standard patterns. Each one you pick becomes a step that is already filled in.

## Example: add getPokemon

<Steps>
1. Open an empty workflow of the Pokédex collection. The canvas shows the card **Build your workflow**.
2. Click **Add the first step**, or press **/**.
3. Type `pokemon/{` and click the row `GET /pokemon/{idOrName}`.
</Steps>

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="In the app">

The canvas gets the card `getPokemon`, with a **GET** method and the path `/pokemon/{idOrName}`. Its panel opens on the right. **Start** now says **1 input**, and the parameter shows the chip **← idOrName · Start**.

![The Design tab with the getPokemon card and its panel open over the canvas](/media/en/builder.webp)

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="In the YAML">

```yaml title="What Luvin writes" {1,5-6,7-8}
- stepId: getPokemon
  operationId: $sourceDescriptions.pokeapi.getPokemon
  parameters:
    - name: idOrName
      in: path
      value: $inputs.idOrName
  successCriteria:
    - condition: $statusCode == 200
```

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Before you start

**＋ Step** lists the operations of the OpenAPI and AsyncAPI contracts of your collection, and its requests. If it says **The collection has no OpenAPI or AsyncAPI contracts. Add one to see its operations here.**, do one of these first:

- Add a contract. See [Add a contract](/contracts/add/).
- Use the requests you already have instead. See [Build from saved requests](/design/from-requests/).

## The ＋ Step search

Click **＋ Step**, or press **/** anywhere on the canvas. A floating menu opens with a search box, **Search operation…**, and these groups:

| Group | What it has | When it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Suggested · use data you already have** | Up to four operations with a parameter named like an output of a step that is already there. Each says why, for example **uses id from step 1**. | Before you type, when there are matches |
| **Patterns · standard Arazzo** | **Go through every page**, **Wait until it finishes** and **Sign in and use the token**. See [Standard Arazzo patterns](/design/patterns/). | When there are operations or requests |
| **Collection requests** | The saved requests, by folder. | When the collection has requests |
| **Workflows in this Arazzo** | The other workflows of the same file, called as one step. | When the file has other workflows |
| **From other Arazzo files** | The workflows of the other Arazzo files of the collection. | When there are other files |
| One group per contract | Its operations, with method, path and summary. A contract that this Arazzo does not use yet is tagged **new**: adding one of its operations adds the contract to the file's sources. | Always |

The search looks at the path, the operation id, the summary and the tags of each operation, and at the name and URL of each request.

<Steps>
1. Type to filter, or move with ↑ and ↓.
2. Click a row, or press Enter. Enter in the search box adds the first match.
</Steps>

To close without adding, press **Esc** or click outside the menu. The foot of the menu reminds you: **↵ add the first one** and **Esc close**.

Operations of AsyncAPI contracts are in the same list, tagged **SEND** or **RECEIVE**. Adding one writes a messaging step. See [Messaging steps](/design/messaging/#add-a-messaging-step).

![The ＋ Step search of the Pokédex: suggestions, the patterns, the workflows of other Arazzo files and the operations of the pokeapi contract](/media/en/design-palette.webp)

## Where the new step goes

| How | Where the step goes |
|---|---|
| **＋ Step** with no step selected | At the end of the happy path: right after the step that ends it with `done`, which moves to the new step. Without steps for errors, that is the end of the workflow. The menu is titled **Add a step**. |
| **＋ Step** with a step selected | Right after that step. The menu is titled **Insert a step**. With **Start** selected, it goes first. |
| The **+** on an arrow of the canvas | Between the two cards that arrow joins. See [Insert a step](/design/insert-step/). |
| Drag a request from the sidebar tree onto the canvas | Below the step you drop it under. Dropped above the first step, it becomes the first one. |

After you add it, the new step is selected, its panel opens on the right and the canvas moves to keep it in view.

## An empty workflow

An empty workflow shows the card **Build your workflow** in the middle of the canvas:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add the first step** | Opens the ＋ Step search. |
| **Build from the collection's requests** | Opens the search and keeps it open: each request you click is added at the end, in order, and Luvin proposes the connections. It shows when the collection has requests. See [Build from saved requests](/design/from-requests/). |

Below them it says **You can also drag a request from the tree on the left.**

## What a new step gets automatically

Luvin reads the contract and fills in the step by rules. Nothing is guessed by AI.

| Part | What Luvin puts |
|---|---|
| Name | The operation id of the contract, for example `getPokemon`. Without an operation id, it is built from the method and the path, for example `getPokemonById`. If the name is taken, a number is added: `getPokemon2`. |
| Description | The summary of the operation, or its method and path if it has none. |
| Success criterion | The lowest 2xx status the contract documents for the operation, for example 201 for a "create". If the contract documents no 2xx, 200. |
| Required parameters | Each one gets a source: a previous step, an input the workflow already has, or a new input created for it. Optional parameters are left out. |
| Body | Each required field gets a source the same way. If the contract marks no field as required, every simple field outside lists becomes an optional input with the contract's example (up to 25 fields; beyond that, none). |

Two things are **not** added for you:

- **Outputs**, apart from the ones the connections need. See [Outputs](/design/outputs/).
- **Routes**. The card lists, in red, each error status the contract documents and nothing handles, so you choose what to do. See [Success and errors](/design/success-and-errors/).

### An example

Start with an empty workflow in the Pokédex collection.

<Steps>
1. Click **＋ Step** and choose `GET /pokemon/{idOrName}`. You get the step `getPokemon`. Nothing provides `idOrName` yet, so **Start** now lists a new input, `idOrName`, and the step uses it.
2. With `getPokemon` selected, click **＋ Step** and choose `GET /pokemon-species/{idOrName}`. You get the step `getPokemonSpecies`. Its `idOrName` shows the chip **← id · from 1** with a `?` in front: Luvin connected it to the `id` the first step returns, and `getPokemon` now has an output called `id`.
</Steps>

The `?` means Luvin is less sure about that connection. [Connect data between steps](/design/wire-data/) explains the rules and how to change a source.

![The workflow after adding two operations: Start has the new input idOrName, getPokemon reads it and gives id, and getPokemonSpecies shows the chip ← id · from 1](/media/en/design-auto-wired.webp)

The panel of the new step opens at once. Its `idOrName` reads the output `id` of `getPokemon`:

![The panel of getPokemonSpecies right after adding it, on the Data tab: idOrName comes from id of step 1](/media/en/design-new-step-panel.webp)

## Other things you can add

| From | What it adds | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **Collection requests** | A saved request, sent exactly as it is. | [Build from saved requests](/design/from-requests/) |
| **Workflows in this Arazzo** or **From other Arazzo files** | Another workflow, called as one step. | [Workflows that use other workflows](/design/dependencies/) |
| **Patterns · standard Arazzo** | A step with its routes already written: pages, polling or a login. | [Standard Arazzo patterns](/design/patterns/) |
| Copy and paste | Steps copied from this workflow or another one. | [Copy, duplicate and extract steps](/design/copy-and-extract/) |

Reusables are not added as steps: they are applied to a step. See [Reusables](/design/reusables/).

### Requests and their required fields

A saved request has no contract that says which of its fields the API requires. Adding one does not ask anything: its card shows **required not marked**, and a bar above the canvas gathers those steps, with **Mark**. See [Mark the required fields of a request](/design/required-fields/).

## If the canvas says the workflow is missing data

A bar titled **This workflow is missing data** appears when a step uses an input the workflow does not declare, or when the contract asks for data a step does not send. This happens, for example, after a contract changes.

Click **Complete**. Luvin declares the missing inputs or connects the data to an earlier step, without redoing the steps. **Undo** reverts it.

![The bar This workflow is missing data above the canvas, with the Complete button](/media/en/design-gaps-bar.webp)

See [Complete missing data](/design/complete-gaps/).
