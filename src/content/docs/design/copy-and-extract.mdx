Arazzo is made to compose: a step can call another workflow and receive its outputs. On the canvas you can select several steps and copy them to another workflow, duplicate them, or turn a group of steps into a workflow of its own, all without touching the YAML.

## Example: reuse the weakness and the ability

In **Pokemon card**, steps 3 and 4 read the weakness of the main type and the first ability. You want them in a workflow of their own, to call from other workflows.

<Steps>
1. Click step 3. Hold **Shift** and click step 4. A bar appears over the canvas: **2 steps selected**.
2. Click **⇪ Extract to a workflow**. A window opens: **Extract 2 steps to a workflow**.
3. In **Name of the new workflow**, type `Weakness and ability`.
4. Read what will change, then click **Extract**.
</Steps>

Steps 3 and 4 move to a new workflow at the end of the file. In their place, **Pokemon card** has one step that calls it. The steps after it, and the outputs of the workflow, now read from that step.

## Select several steps

| How | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Shift** + click, or ⌘ + click (Ctrl + click) | Adds the step to the selection, or takes it out |
| Right-click › **Add to the selection** | The same, from the menu of the step |
| **⌘A** (Ctrl+A) on the canvas | Selects every step |
| A plain click on a step | Selects only that step |
| **Esc**, or **Cancel** in the bar | Stops selecting |

The selected cards are highlighted. With two or more, a bar floats over the canvas:

| In the bar | What it does |
|---|---|
| **N steps selected** · **⇧ click to add or remove** | How many and how to change them |
| **⎘ Copy** (⌘C) | Copies them |
| **⧉ Duplicate** | Pastes a copy right after the last selected step |
| **⇪ Extract to a workflow** | Moves them to a new workflow. Only for consecutive steps |
| **× Remove** | Removes them, after a confirmation. See [Remove a step](/design/organize/#remove-a-step) |
| **Cancel** | Stops selecting |

## Copy and paste

<Steps>
1. Select one or more steps.
2. Press **⌘C** (Ctrl+C), or click **⎘ Copy** in the bar, or right-click a card and choose **Copy**. A notice says **2 steps copied: ⌘V pastes them after the selected step, in this workflow or another.**
3. Open the workflow where they go, in this file or another one.
4. Select the step they go after, and press **⌘V** (Ctrl+V), or right-click it and choose **Paste after this step**. With no step selected, they go at the end of the workflow.
</Steps>

The steps are pasted with their parameters, criteria, outputs and routes, and stay selected.

What Luvin copies lives while the app is open. It is also copied to the system clipboard as YAML, so you can paste it into a text file or the YAML tab.

### What changes when you paste

- **Ids that already exist get a number.** A pasted `getPokemon` becomes `getPokemon_2`, then `getPokemon_3`. Inside what you pasted, the data, the routes and the `dependsOn` follow the new ids.
- **Data that exists in the destination stays connected.** A value that reads an input or a step that the destination workflow has keeps working.
- **Data that does not exist is marked.** A value that reads a step that is not there shows a red chip, **missing**. An input the workflow does not declare also shows red; its tooltip says **This workflow has no input «…»: add it in Start or connect another value.** Give those values a source. See [Connect data between steps](/design/wire-data/).
- **Routes to steps that are not there** are flagged in [To review](/design/review/), like any broken route.

⌘C and ⌘V copy text as usual while text is selected or the cursor is in a field.

## Duplicate

Select one or more steps and press **⌘D** (Ctrl+D), or click **⧉ Duplicate** in the bar, or right-click a card and choose **Duplicate**. The copies go right after the last selected step, with new ids, as when you paste.

## Extract to a workflow

Extracting turns consecutive steps into a new workflow of the same file, and leaves in their place one step that calls it.

<Steps>
1. Select the steps. They must be one after another. With steps that are not, the button is off and says **Only consecutive steps: select steps one after another.** To extract a single step, right-click it and choose **Extract to a workflow…**.
2. Click **⇪ Extract to a workflow** in the bar, or **Extract to a workflow…** in the menu of a card.
3. Type the **Name of the new workflow**. It starts as **New workflow**.
4. Read the window, then click **Extract**, or **Cancel**.
</Steps>

![The window Extract 2 steps to a workflow: the name of the new workflow, its inputs and outputs, and how the YAML changes](/media/en/design-extract-dialog.webp)

Nothing is written until you click **Extract**. The window shows:

| Section | What it says |
|---|---|
| The first line | The id of the new workflow and of the step that calls it: **The steps move to the workflow «…», in this file. In their place goes the step «…», which calls it.** |
| **Inputs (what they received from outside)** | Each value the steps read from the workflow's inputs or from earlier steps, with where it came from, for example `mainType ← $steps.getPokemon.outputs.mainType` |
| **Outputs (what is used afterwards)** | Each value the later steps or the workflow outputs read from the group, and **used by** whom |
| **Routes that are removed** | Routes of the group that go to a step that stays outside. They are removed |
| Notes | What works differently once inside the new workflow (see below) |
| **How it looks in Arazzo** | The YAML of the step that goes **In place of the steps** and of the workflow that goes **At the end of the file** |

After **Extract**, the step that calls the new workflow is selected. One **↶ Undo** puts everything back.

### What changes inside the new workflow

- **The rules for every step** (the ones in **Start**) are copied to the new workflow, so its steps keep them. Rules that go to a step that stays outside are not copied.
- **A route that ends successfully** now ends the new workflow, and the main one goes on with the next step.
- **A route that continues in another workflow** does so inside the new workflow, and when it ends the main one goes on with the next step.
- **A route that fails and ends** works as before: the new workflow fails, the step that calls it fails, and the main workflow ends.
- **A route from outside that went into the middle of the group** now goes to the step that calls the new workflow, which starts with the first extracted step.

The new workflow always goes to the same file.

## See the steps of a workflow that a step calls

A step that calls a workflow of the same file has a link on its card: **▸ See its 2 steps** (or **▸ See its step**). Click it to list those steps, with their method, without leaving the canvas. **▾ Hide its steps** folds them. Workflows of other files are not listed.

To open the called workflow, click it in the sidebar. See [Workflows that use other workflows](/design/dependencies/).
