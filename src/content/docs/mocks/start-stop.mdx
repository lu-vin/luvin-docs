A mock answers only while it runs. Starting it opens its port on your computer, and stopping it closes the port. This page covers both, the **Port** and **Delay (ms)** fields, and what to do when a mock does not start.

## Example: start the Pokédex mock

<Steps>
1. At the top of the sidebar, click the **Mock servers** icon. In its panel, click **Pokédex mock**. Its tab opens.
2. Press **Start**. The button reads **Starting…** for a moment.
3. The top of the tab now shows `http://127.0.0.1:4010`, **Copy URL** and **Stop**. The dot next to the name is green.
4. Press **Copy URL** and paste the URL in a request or in cURL. The button reads **✓ Copied** for a moment.
5. When you are done, press **Stop**.
</Steps>

## Start and stop

| From | Start | Stop |
|---|---|---|
| The mock tab | **Start**, at the top right. | **Stop**, next to the URL. |
| The **Mock servers** panel of the sidebar | **⋯** menu of the mock → **Start**. | **⋯** menu of the mock → **Stop**. |

The **⋯** menu of the sidebar also has **Copy URL** while the mock runs.

![The ⋯ menu of the running Shop mock in the Mock servers panel of the sidebar, with Stop, Copy URL and Delete mock; the panel's icon at the top has a green dot](/media/en/mocks-sidebar-menu.webp)

### What you see while it runs

| Where | Running | Stopped |
|---|---|---|
| The top of the mock tab | A green dot, the URL, **Copy URL** and **Stop**. | A gray dot and **Start**. |
| The **Mock servers** panel of the sidebar | A green dot and the port, for example `:4010`. Hover over it: *Running on port 4010*. | A gray dot and the port. Hover: *Stopped*. |
| The **Mock servers** icon at the top of the sidebar | A green dot on the icon, even when another panel is open. | No dot, when no mock of the collection is running. |
| The tab bar | The tab of the mock shows the same dot. | |
| The command palette (⌘K) | The mock, under **Mock servers**, says *Running on port 4010*. | It says *Stopped*. |

In the palette, choosing a mock opens its tab.

### Changes apply while it runs

A running mock answers what you change at once, without a restart: its own routes, the **Delay (ms)**, the **Responses from** list, the saved examples of the requests and the contract. Only a change of **Port** restarts it.

## The port

The mock answers at `http://127.0.0.1:` followed by its port.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Port of a new mock | 4010, or the next number no other mock of the collection uses: 4011, 4012… Luvin does not check other programs until you press **Start**. |
| When a change applies | When you press Enter or leave the field. Esc puts back the previous value. |
| Valid values | 1 to 65535. Any other value goes back to the previous one. |
| With the mock running | It restarts on the new port. If the new port fails, the mock stays stopped and the tab shows why. |

<Aside type="caution">
The URLs of the mock environment do not follow the port on their own. After you change the port, press **Update environment** in the mock tab. See [Use a mock with requests and workflows](/mocks/with-workflows/).
</Aside>

## The delay

**Delay (ms)** makes the mock wait before each response, from 0 to 120000 ms (2 minutes). It goes up in steps of 100 with the arrows, and applies at once. Use it to see how a workflow behaves with a slow API, or to check a timeout.

- A route can have its own **Delay (ms)**, which replaces the delay of the mock. See [Responses](/mocks/responses/).
- The delay applies only to calls that a route answers. A `404` without a route and a `403` for a wrong host answer at once.
- The **Log** shows the duration of each call, delay included.

## When it does not start

If the mock cannot start, the tab says why, in red:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *That port is already in use. Pick another one and start it again.* | Another program, or another mock of the collection, uses that port. Change the **Port** and press **Start**. |
| *The system does not allow that port. Try one above 1024.* | Your system reserves the low ports. Use one above 1024, such as 4010. |
| *The port must be a number between 1 and 65535.* | Write a valid number. |
| Another message | It is the message of the system. Try another port. |

## When a mock stops on its own

A mock does not keep running without Luvin. It stops when you:

- open another collection, or close the collection;
- reload the window;
- quit the app.

After a reload, Luvin also stops any mock that was left running, so none answers without you seeing it. Its **Log** is emptied. Press **Start** again when you need it.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Why, and what to do |
|---|---|
| **Start** is disabled. | You are in the browser version. Open the collection in the desktop app. |
| Calls to `http://localhost:4010` fail, but `http://127.0.0.1:4010` works. | The mock listens only on `127.0.0.1`, and on some systems `localhost` goes to the IPv6 address. Use the URL that **Copy URL** gives. |
| Your requests still go to the old port. | The mock environment keeps the old URL. Press **Update environment**. |
| The mock stopped while you were working. | You changed collection, reloaded or quit. Start it again. |
| A company proxy is configured. | It does not matter: `localhost` and `127.0.0.1` always connect directly. See [Network settings](/get-started/network/). |
