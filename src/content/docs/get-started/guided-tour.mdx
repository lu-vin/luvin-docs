The guided tour shows Luvin on a real example, one highlighted area of the screen at a time. It is split into seven short sections of 1 or 2 minutes each, so you see only what you need. Start with **Workflows**: the other sections end by showing how their feature is used in a workflow.

![The tour card Workflows · step 2 of 7, Collections, workflows and steps, next to the highlighted collection tree in the sidebar, with Exit tour, Back and Next](/media/en/tour.webp)

## Start the tour

| Where | What to click | What opens |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome screen | The **Guided tour** card: *Start with workflows (2 minutes) and continue with short sections* | The **Workflows** section |
| The banner **You’re viewing an example.** | **Start the tour** | The **Workflows** section |
| Status bar, at the bottom | **Tour** | The section picker |
| **Preferences** → **About** | **Start the tour**, next to **Guided tour** | The section picker |

The banner appears when you open an example: *Want a walkthrough? The tour starts with workflows (about 2 minutes) and continues by sections.* Its **✕** has the tooltip **Don’t show again**. Luvin remembers it on this computer, so the banner does not come back for any example. It does not appear while the tour is running.

See [A tour of the interface](/get-started/interface/) for the status bar and Preferences.

## Choose a section

The section picker is titled **What do you want to see?**, with the line *Short sections on the Pokédex. Each one ends by showing how it is used in a workflow, the heart of Luvin.*

<Steps>
1. Click **Tour** in the status bar.
2. Click a section. The first one, **Workflows**, has the badge **Start here** until you see it.
</Steps>

Each section shows its number, its title, a summary, its length and **View**. Close the picker with **×**, **Esc** or a click outside it.

| Section | What it shows | Steps | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Workflows**](/get-started/guided-tour-sections/#1-workflows) | A workflow end to end: inputs, cases, run and which data went from one step to the next. | 7 | 2 min |
| [**Design a workflow**](/get-started/guided-tour-sections/#2-design-a-workflow) | The canvas, the palette, the YAML, workflows that depend on others and events. | 5 | 2 min |
| [**Contracts and environments**](/get-started/guided-tour-sections/#3-contracts-and-environments) | The contract, trying an operation, environments and secrets, and per-API authorization. It adds a second collection, **PayPal**. | 6 | 2 min |
| [**Requests**](/get-started/guided-tour-sections/#4-requests) | A standalone request, its variables and checks, and how it becomes a workflow step. | 3 | 2 min |
| [**Mock servers**](/get-started/guided-tour-sections/#5-mock-servers) | A mock from the contract and the workflow running against it, without the real API. | 3 | 1 min |
| [**Runner and performance**](/get-started/guided-tour-sections/#6-runner-and-performance) | Several workflows with data, rules to pick from a list and the load test. | 4 | 2 min |
| [**History and Dev Tools**](/get-started/guided-tour-sections/#7-history-and-dev-tools) | Going back to a workflow run, Network, search and preferences. | 5 | 1 min |

You can take the sections in any order. Each one gets the screen ready by itself. [Tour sections](/get-started/guided-tour-sections/) describes each step of each section.

When you finish a section with one of the buttons of its last card, the picker shows **✓** instead of its number, and **View** becomes **View again**. See [Sections you have seen](/get-started/guided-tour-workspace/#sections-you-have-seen).

![The section picker What do you want to see? with the seven sections and their minutes; Workflows shows ✓ and View again, and Delete the tour workspace is at the bottom](/media/en/tour-sections.webp)

## Move through a section

The card shows the section and the step, for example **Workflows · step 3 of 7**, and a progress bar. While a step gets the screen ready, the card says **Getting ready…** and **Next** waits.

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Next step | **Next** or the → key |
| Previous step | **Back** or the ← key. The first step has no **Back**. |
| Repeat a step | **Back**, then **Next**. The step gets the screen ready again, and runs again if it runs something. |
| Leave the tour | **Exit tour** |

The highlighted area follows the screen as panels open or move. When a step has no area to highlight, or the area is not there, the card goes to the middle and the rest of the screen is dimmed.

The tour switches the panels of the sidebar for you: each step shows the **Collections**, **Environments**, **History** or **Mock servers** panel it needs.

You can use the app at any time while the tour is open. The tour does not block it.

## The end of a section

The last card of each section has three buttons instead of **Back**, **Next** and **Exit tour**:

| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Next: Design a workflow** | Starts the next section, in the order of the picker. The last section, **History and Dev Tools**, does not have it. |
| **All sections** | Opens the section picker. |
| **Finish** | Closes the tour. |

Any of the three marks the section as seen.

## Where the tour works

The tour opens its own workspace, **Pokédex (tour)**, kept in the storage of the app, not in a folder on disk. If you have another workspace open, Luvin closes it first. What you change during the tour stays in that workspace until you delete it.

The tour runs things by itself, as you would: it opens a workflow, a tab, and presses **Run**. Some steps call the real PokéAPI and need internet. The PayPal example runs in **Simulation**. A load test is never started.

|---|---|

In the **Simple (product)** interface, **History and Dev Tools** skips its Dev Tools step, because that interface has no Dev Tools.
