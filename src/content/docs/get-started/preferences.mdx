**Preferences** holds the settings that belong to you and to this computer, not to a workspace: language, role, interface, theme, history and how requests go out to the network. Open it when Luvin shows too much (or too little) technical detail, when you want another language or theme, or when your company uses a proxy or certificates.

![The Preferences dialog open on General: the sections on the left, the Requests settings with their defaults, and Language and Interface below them](/media/en/preferences.webp)

## Open Preferences

<Steps>

1. Click the gear icon at the bottom left of the window (the status bar), or press ⌘, (Ctrl+,).
2. The **Preferences** dialog opens over the app, on **General**. The sections are on the left.
3. Change what you need. Each change applies at once; there is no save button. Text and number fields save when you leave the field or press Enter.
4. Press Esc, click the **✕** or click outside the dialog to close it.

</Steps>

Luvin remembers every setting on this computer. It does not save them in your workspace or in your files.

## The sections

| Section | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **General** | **Requests**, **Application**, **Workspaces** and **History** (see below). |
| **Theme** | **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. **System** follows the macOS or Windows appearance and switches on its own. |
| **Shortcuts** | The keyboard shortcuts, in groups: **General**, **Tabs** and **Workflow canvas**. The full list is in [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts/). |
| **Certificates** | CA certificates and client certificates. |
| **Proxy** | System proxy, environment variables and a custom proxy. |
| **About** | The version of Luvin, **Privacy** and the **Guided tour**, with **Start the tour**. |

## General

### Requests

How requests go out: **Request timeout**, **Max response size**, **SSL certificate verification**, **Follow redirects**, **Max redirects** and **Automatic cookies**. Each one, with its default, is explained in [Network settings](/get-started/network/).

### Application

| Setting | Options | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Language** | **Español**, **English** | Changes every label of the app. The first time, Luvin follows the language of your system. |
| **Interface** | **Full (development)**, **Simple (product)** | How much technical detail Luvin shows. |
| **Your role** | **Product Manager / Owner**, **Business or partners**, **QA / Testing**, **Development**, **Architecture**, **Other** | Also sets the interface, as the next table shows. Empty (**—**) until you choose one. |

#### Role and interface go together

Choosing a role changes the interface. Changing only the interface does not change the role.

| Role | Interface Luvin selects |
|---|---|
| **Product Manager / Owner** | **Simple (product)** |
| **Business or partners** | **Simple (product)** |
| **QA / Testing** | **Full (development)** |
| **Development** | **Full (development)** |
| **Architecture** | **Full (development)** |
| **Other** | **Full (development)** |

The welcome screen offers the same choice the first time. See [A tour of the interface](/get-started/interface/).

### Workspaces

**Location for new workspaces** says where new workspaces are suggested: `Documents/Luvin` until you click **Choose…** and pick another folder. **Reset** goes back to the default.

### History

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save history** | On by default. Keeps the requests you send and your workflow runs, per collection and only on this computer. Environment secrets and credentials are saved masked. Turn it off and nothing new is saved. |
| **Clear all history** | Clears the history of every collection on this computer. Click **Clear history**, then confirm with **Clear history** in *Clear the history of every collection?* It says **History cleared** when done. |

![General further down: Language, Interface and Your role under Application, Location for new workspaces with Choose…, and Save history and Clear history under History](/media/en/prefs-general.webp)

## The theme, without opening Preferences

The sun or moon icon next to the gear, in the status bar, changes the theme in one click. Each click goes to the next one: **System**, **Light**, **Dark**, and back to **System**. Its tooltip says the current theme and the next one.

## About

**Privacy** says where your data lives: *Everything runs on your computer. Requests go out from the app, never through a server of ours. Your session is saved without credentials.* With a workspace open: *Every change is saved in the workspace files automatically. Secrets and credentials are encrypted on this computer, never in the files.* See [The guided tour](/get-started/guided-tour/) for **Start the tour**.

## Example: a product manager opens Pokemon card

Open the Pokédex example with each interface and compare the same workflow.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Full (development)">

  - The workflow opens on **Design**. **Overview** is the last tab.
  - Steps are called `getPokemon`, `getSpecies`, and each one shows its method, for example `GET`.
  - A step panel lists `onSuccess`, `onFailure` and `Outputs`.
  - **Dev Tools** is in the status bar.

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Simple (product)">

  - The workflow opens on **Overview**, now the first tab. **Design** comes next.
  - Steps are called **Get pokemon**, **Get species**, with no method.
  - A step panel says **If it goes well**, **If it goes wrong** and **Data obtained**.
  - The criterion `$statusCode == 200` reads **The API responds 200 (OK)**.
  - **Dev Tools** is gone.

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## What the Simple interface changes

It changes how things are shown. It does not change what Luvin does: the same cases run and the same requests are sent.

| Where | In Simple |
|---|---|
| Status bar | The **Dev Tools** button disappears, and the panel stays hidden even if it was open. See [Dev Tools](/dev-tools/overview/). |
| Terminal | **Open in Terminal** is off, with the hint **not in simple mode**. |
| Tabs of a workflow | **Overview** goes first. The workflow opens on it. |
| Sidebar and tabs | Steps use a readable name (`getPokemon` becomes **Get pokemon**) and have no method badge. |
| The canvas (**Design**) | Step cards show readable names, the description of the step and the criteria in words. |
| The step tab | No technical line (operationId, timeout, dependsOn). Values show in words, and the expression appears when you point at them. The titles are **If it goes well**, **If it goes wrong** and **Data obtained**. |
| Results | Step names are readable and the request line is hidden. Criteria are in words, and a passed one shows **—** instead of detail. The outputs tab is **Data obtained**. |
| Dialog to choose from a list | Step names are readable. See [Lists](/test/lists/). |
| Guided tour | Skips the **Dev Tools** stop. |

<Aside type="note">
The YAML, the inputs, the cases, the environments and the contracts are the same in both interfaces. Switch to **Full (development)** whenever you need the technical detail.
</Aside>

## Switch the interface

<Steps>

1. Open **Preferences**.
2. In **General**, next to **Interface**, click **Simple (product)** or **Full (development)**. The hint under the name says what each one means.
3. Look at the app behind the dialog: it changes at once.

</Steps>
